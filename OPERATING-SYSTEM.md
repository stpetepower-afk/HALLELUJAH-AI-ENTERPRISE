# OPERATING-SYSTEM.md

Roles & Workflow

- Fable: Chief Architect & product definition. Defines system architecture, ADRs, and technical priorities.
- Sol: Engineering lead / build automation. Implements code, CI, and deployment pipelines.
- Gemini: Research & knowledge agent. Produces market research and documentation templates.

Workflow
1. Fable defines requirements, ADRs, and high-level designs.
2. Sol translates into implementation tasks, CI configs, and infra as code.
3. Gemini prepares knowledge artifacts, research, and supporting materials.
4. Continuous review loop via PRs and ADRs.
